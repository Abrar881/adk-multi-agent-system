Acme Property Services — Company Overview

Acme Property Services is a residential property inventory and check-in/check-out company serving landlords, letting agents, and tenants across Greater London and the South East.

Founded in 2015, we provide independent, impartial property condition reports used by landlords and agents to protect both parties at the start and end of tenancies.

Services include inventory reports, check-in reports, check-out reports, mid-term inspections, and professional cleaning.
