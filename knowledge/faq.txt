Acme Property Services — Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I book an inventory?
A: Email info@acme-property-services.com with the property address, type (furnished/unfurnished), and preferred date/time. We will confirm availability and send a booking confirmation.

Q: How long does an inventory take?
A: Typically 1–2 hours for a 1-bed flat, 2–3 hours for a 3-bed house. Furnished properties take longer.

Q: Do I need to be present for the inventory?
A: Not for a standalone inventory report. For a check-in, the tenant should be present to sign the report.

Q: What happens at check-out?
A: Our clerk compares the property condition against the original inventory. Any differences are noted with photographs. The check-out report is evidence for deposit dispute resolution.

Q: Can I use your reports for a deposit dispute?
A: Yes. Our reports are prepared to APIP (Association of Professional Inventory Providers) standards and are widely accepted by deposit schemes (TDS, DPS, mydeposits).

Q: What is your cancellation policy?
A: Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice incur a 50% fee. No-shows are charged in full.

Q: Do you cover properties outside London?
A: Yes, with a surcharge. See our coverage areas document for details.

Q: How is payment handled?
A: We invoice after the appointment. Payment is due within 14 days. We accept bank transfer and card.

Q: Do you offer same-day reports?
A: Yes, with a £25 surcharge. Please request this at time of booking.
