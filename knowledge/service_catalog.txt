Acme Property Services — Service Catalog

INVENTORY REPORTS
A full written and photographic record of the property condition at the start of a tenancy. Includes all rooms, fixtures, fittings, furniture, and appliances.
Turnaround: report delivered within 24 hours.

CHECK-IN REPORTS
Tenant walkthrough with the clerk present. Tenant signs to confirm agreement with the inventory. Meters read, keys issued and documented.

CHECK-OUT REPORTS
End-of-tenancy inspection comparing condition against the original inventory. Notes any damage, missing items, or cleaning issues for deposit dispute resolution.

MID-TERM INSPECTIONS
Interim property visits to confirm tenant compliance with the tenancy agreement. Photographs taken, report delivered within 24 hours.

PROFESSIONAL CLEANING
End-of-tenancy deep cleaning service. Includes kitchen appliances, bathrooms, carpets, windows (interior). Certificate issued on completion.

ENERGY PERFORMANCE CERTIFICATES (EPC)
Accredited assessors produce EPC ratings (A–G) required for all rental properties in England and Wales.

FLOOR PLANS
Measured floor plans to RICS standards. Useful for lettings listings and property management records.
